# 0048. Cohort of 34 at 2,000 forks with three structural rules

Date: 2026-10-22

## Status

accepted

## Context

ADR 0047 selected 31 repositories at 3,000 forks by a purpose review. Reviewing the retained repositories more closely on 2026-10-22 showed three patterns that do not match the population of ADR 0011. `BillionsNetwork/verified-agent-identity` has 6,589 forks but 757 stars (8.7 forks per star; every other retained repository lies between 0.05 and 0.31) and its forks arrived in bursts of dozens per minute from throwaway accounts, the signature of an incentivised forking campaign; `mcncarl/yichen-skills` gained 100 forks in the two days before the review at 0.48 forks per star. `mukul975/Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills` (818 packages) and `alirezarezvani/claude-skills` (398 packages, several vendored) are catalogs rather than maintained skill sets. `JuliusBrussee/caveman` and eight further repositories ship a standalone product, a proxy, engine, CLI, web app or service, beside their skills, or are workflows that a user forks and fills in. Because the review already covered every candidate with 2,000 forks or more, the threshold can be lowered to 2,000 without new review cost.

## Decision

Lower the fork threshold to 2,000 and add three structural rules after the fork threshold and the manifest check:

1. Forks may not exceed stars (`maximum_forks_per_star: 1.0`). More forks than stars signals a fork campaign or a fork to use template. This is not a star minimum, which ADR 0002 declined; in the candidate pool the ratio has a median of about 0.15 and a wide gap up to the campaign and classroom repositories in the hundreds, so the cut is insensitive between 0.5 and 1.0.
2. At most 300 skill packages (`maximum_packages: 300`), counted on the complete frozen tree. Every repository above 300 packages in the pool is a catalog.
3. Skill packages must be the deliverable. Code is allowed inside packages or as scripts, tests, evaluations, hooks and documentation that serve them; a standalone runtime product beside the skills, or a fork and fill workflow, excludes the repository. This rule is a documented judgment: the review table records the directories and code file counts outside the packages as evidence.

Rules 1 and 2 are implemented in screening; rule 3 and the purpose review of ADR 0047 are applied through the review table, whose include rows define `screening.repositories`. The user adjudicated the borderline cases on 2026-10-22 and additionally excluded `alchaincyf/zhangxuefeng-skill` as a package generated by another cohort skill and `Leanmcp/gateway-skills` as a vendor promotional repository; `EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin` stays because its non skill code is packaging tooling, the same pattern as `obra/superpowers`. The cohort is 34 repositories with about 212,000 forks; 19 of them were in the September cohort.

## Alternatives

- **A star minimum such as 20,000**: any value between 1,000 and 28,000 removes the same single repository; the number cannot be justified.
- **Judge catalogs and products case by case without rules**: leaves the boundary implicit; the numeric rules reproduce the same decisions and are reported as filtering steps, and rule 3 keeps its evidence in the table.
- **Keep 3,000**: the review is already done down to 2,000; the lower threshold adds 13 repositories, broadens the maintainer and language mix and keeps the security domain from being a single repository, for about 16 percent more forks.

## Consequences

- Easier: the funnel is one search date, one threshold, four structural rules and one published review table; fork counts inflated by campaigns no longer enter denominators.
- Harder: a repository with a legitimate fork to contribute workflow is excluded; large authored libraries and tool backed skills are out of scope and the paper must say so; rule 3 is a judgment and the review was drafted by an assistant and confirmed by one person.
